CLIMB SMART

🟢 Rotten Mango — dodge it. If it hits you, you lose 1 health.

🟡 Yellow Mango — catch it for +20 score and a small Rush boost.

🥭 Golden Mango — rare premium reward: +40 score and a big Rush boost.

📈 Difficulty — the higher you climb, the mangoes become faster and more numerous.

🌿 Branches — branches are spaced apart and monkeys alternate sides, keeping the opening climb readable.
